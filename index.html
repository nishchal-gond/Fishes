The fish market, live 🐋

Press and drag in the water to steer the big fish — you're the aggressive trader. Coral fish are sell orders and always float above the current price; green fish are buy orders waiting below it. Eat upward and you're buying — the candle climbs. Eat downward and you're selling — it falls. New orders keep arriving around wherever price is, so the whole book — and the spread — follows you.

Drag the big fish to a glowing row to make your first trade.

Glowing rows are the best ask and best bid — markets always trade the best available price first. The gold band between them is the spread: it stretches when you clear a side out, then tightens again as fresh orders rebuild around price.

Your chart so far

Every time you close a candle, it joins the chart — same scale, one candle per period. This is all a price chart ever is.
